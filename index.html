Our models

We don’t just re-explain the lecture. We rebuilt the way 233 is taught.

Chemistry 233 moves fast: mechanisms, stereochemistry, spectroscopy, and “why did that arrow go there?” Our tutors use teaching models we designed around the UBC course — so sessions map to what you’re actually being tested on, not a random organic chem playlist.
